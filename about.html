About

Hi, I'm Brandon.

I write notes and essays about public markets — what they're pricing, what they're missing, and the slow work of figuring out the difference.

New pieces go out on Substack and live in the archive here. Side projects and research artifacts — spreadsheets, scripts, the occasional long PDF — sit on the projects page.

My background is in finance: reading filings, building small models, paying attention to the footnotes most people skip. I started writing publicly because the things I find interesting weren't getting written about the way I wanted to read them.
