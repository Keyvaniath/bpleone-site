About

Through-cycle research, graded in public.

Finance MBA, passed Level I of the CFA Program, former growth equity; my team out of Cal State Fullerton won the 2023 CFA Institute Research Challenge for the Americas region and advanced to the global final in Washington, D.C. I publish independent equity research on semiconductors and AI infrastructure, on the energy and power names that supply them, and on the commodities and macro layer that drives both, with through-cycle valuation as the lens and the question of what the market price already assumes as the starting point of every note.

Most independent finance writing online is either macro narrative or a stock pitch written to be agreed with, so the work here is built closer to a sell-side investment-committee memo with one addition most sell-side notes skip: every rating ships with a published target and the specific number that would prove it wrong, and the track record grades each call in public when that number prints. The coverage table is generated from the notes themselves, which means it cannot show one rating while the research says another, every assumption is published, and the Python and Excel pipeline that builds the workbooks is free to download.

The current book is public, and the next decider is dated

The book of coverage, with the rating, the target and the note behind each name, lives on the coverage page, and the now page keeps a dated snapshot of what the next few weeks will decide. New pieces go out on Substack and stay in the archive here, while the valuation templates and in-browser calculators sit in the toolkit.

Email reaches me fastest

I take serious conversations about public markets from analysts, fund teams, family offices and other writers who read filings carefully, whether that means comparing notes on a name, arguing a thesis, or putting the modelling pipeline to work on something of yours, and email is the fastest route. I do this work because the work is the point.
