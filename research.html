Free data stack for a cyclicals research site.

Widget blueprint

If the source tables above are the inputs, this is the outputs — the specific dashboards, trackers, and metrics each vertical's data becomes once it's wired up. The “Site use” column in each table links straight to the matching widget below.

Oil & energy

  • Oil dashboard. WTI, Brent, WTI–Brent spread, 1M / 3M / 12M change, moving averages, and percentile.
  • Inventory surprise dashboard. EIA crude, gasoline, distillate stocks, refinery utilization, imports, exports, product supplied.
  • Refining margin proxy. Use EIA / FRED gasoline, diesel, and crude series to calculate simple 3-2-1 crack-spread style proxies.
  • Positioning dashboard. CFTC crude managed-money net length, week-over-week change, and percentile.
  • XOM driver bridge. Oil price, gas price, refining utilization, product supplied, capex, buybacks, production, and free cash flow.

Semiconductors

  • Memory pricing tracker. DRAM spot price trend, NAND spot trend if available, 4-week change, 13-week change, and an “up/down streak” counter.
  • Global semi sales tracker. WSTS billings by region, 3-month moving average, YoY change, and cycle percentile.
  • Equipment / capex tracker. SEMI billings, ASML / AMAT / LRCX commentary if you later add transcript parsing, and memory capex commentary.
  • MU fundamentals tracker. Revenue, gross margin, inventory, days inventory, capex, operating cash flow, free cash flow, and management language from SEC filings and Micron IR.
  • HBM tracker. Pull MU earnings transcripts/presentations and tag mentions of HBM, pricing, customer commitments, capacity, supply tightness, and margins.

Shipping & trade

  • Container freight tracker. Freightos, Drewry WCI, SCFI, and lane-specific China-to-U.S. indicators.
  • Port throughput tracker. Monthly TEUs from BTS / LA / LB and YoY change.
  • Trade flow tracker. Census imports/exports by HS code for semiconductors, electronics, crude oil, petroleum products, and energy equipment.
  • Supply-chain stress dashboard. NY Fed GSCPI, container rates, port TEUs, supplier-delivery indexes, and freight changes.

Macro & demand

  • Supply-chain pressure tracker. NY Fed GSCPI level, percentile, and 3-month change — the cleanest single read on whether the global supply chain is loose or tight.
  • Industrial production overlay. FRED semiconductor industrial production indexed against WSTS billings — cross-check whether U.S. domestic production confirms or diverges from global cycle.
  • PMI breadth dashboard. ISM manufacturing PMI plus its sub-indices (new orders, inventories, supplier deliveries) — the demand-side leading indicator that sits underneath every other vertical.

One-stop public research terminal

Eight sources to ship the v1.

This is the smallest set that produces a credible cycle dashboard for semis, energy, and shipping in a weekend — no Bloomberg, no FactSet, no Daloopa, no paid shipping feeds.

  1. EIA API — oil prices, inventories, refinery utilization, product demand.
  2. FRED API — WTI / Brent, macro indicators, industrial production, easy chartable history.
  3. SEC EDGAR API — MU / XOM financials, filings, filing alerts.
  4. WSTS Historical Billings Report — monthly semi cycle data.
  5. Census Trade API — import/export flows by HS code.
  6. BTS Socrata API — port TEU data.
  7. CFTC COT — crude positioning.
  8. TrendForce DRAM page — memory price direction.
