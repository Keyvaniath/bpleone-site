How I work

Every published pitch ships with the workbook that produced it. Every assumption is named; every fallback is transparent. Where the data is absent — a private name, a missing fiscal year, a peer with a non-comparable accounting policy — the model says "N/A" instead of silently filling in a magic number. This is the bar I learned at the CFA Institute Research Challenge and I hold every piece I publish to it.
